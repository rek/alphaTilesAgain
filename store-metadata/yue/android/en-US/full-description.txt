學廣話 (AlphaTiles Cantonese) helps children and new readers learn to read Cantonese written in Traditional Chinese characters, as used in Hong Kong.

Every character and word is spoken aloud in Cantonese, so learners connect what they hear with what they see. Short, playful games build skills one step at a time.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• More than 25 short games, grouped into steps from easiest to hardest, with a guide to where to start
• Hear each character and word spoken in Cantonese
• Match sounds, pictures and words
• Build words from character tiles
• Practise writing characters stroke by stroke
• Trackers and celebrations reward progress
• Several players can share one device, each with their own avatar and progress
• App screens in Traditional Chinese (Hong Kong)

SAFE FOR CHILDREN
• No ads and no in-app purchases
• No account or sign-up
• Player names and progress stay on the device
• All games, pictures and audio are built in and work offline

ABOUT ALPHATILES
學廣話 is built with AlphaTiles, an open-source literacy game generator for minority and community languages. A language community provides its word list, sounds, pictures and recordings, and AlphaTiles turns them into tile-based reading games.

Feedback and source code: https://github.com/rek/alphaTilesAgain
